# CorpusLite: Play Console content rating (IARC questionnaire)

Play Console → Policy → App content → Content rating → Start questionnaire.

## Start

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Email address | A support address you check. IARC sends the rating certificate there. |
| Category | **All Other App Types** |

Pick "All Other App Types" because CorpusLite is a business tool: billing, stock and accounts.

- It is not a game.
- It is not "Social or Communication". The only chat is with your support team, and users don't message each other.
- It is not "Reference, News or Educational".

## Questions

Every content question is **No**.

| Topic | Question (in short) | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Violence | Violence, blood, gore, weapons? | **No** | |
| Fear | Scary or horror content? | **No** | |
| Sexuality | Nudity, sexual content or innuendo? | **No** | |
| Language | Swearing or crude language? | **No** | |
| Controlled substances | References to or use of drugs, alcohol or tobacco? | **No** | The app has none. A shop may sell such goods and enter them as items, but that is the shop's own business record, not content the app shows. |
| Crude humour | Crude or toilet humour? | **No** | |
| Gambling | Real or simulated gambling? | **No** | There is no betting, no lottery and no chance-based rewards. Loyalty points are earned by purchases, not by chance. |
| User interaction | Can users interact or exchange content with other users (chat, voice, images)? | **No** | Staff of one shop work on the same records, but they don't message each other. Support chat is with your own team. Sharing a bill on WhatsApp goes out through Android's share screen to another app. |
| Location sharing | Does the app share the user's current location with other users? | **No** | The app has no location permission. |
| Digital purchases | Can users buy digital goods? | **No** | Plans are paid on the website, not in the app. |
| Unrestricted internet | Is the app a web browser or search engine, or does it give unrestricted web access? | **No** | It only talks to your own server. |
| Personal info | Does the app share personal information with third parties? | **No** | The answer matches the Data safety form. |

**If you are asked about AI-generated content:** answer **Yes** if a question asks whether the app generates content with AI. The AI assistant and the help bot write short text answers about the shop's own records. They don't make images, voices or content that is shared publicly. Users can report a wrong answer through Help → Talk to a person.

## Expected result

All questions are "No", so the expected rating is the lowest in every region:

- **IARC (most countries):** 3+
- **PEGI (Europe):** 3
- **ESRB (North America):** Everyone
- **USK (Germany):** 0

Plus the equivalents in other regions.

There are no content descriptors. Users interact with each other: **No**. In-app purchases: **No**.

The *Target audience* answer (**18 and over**) is a separate section of App content. Because the app is for business owners and staff, not for children, its everyone-safe rating doesn't put it in the Families programme.

## When to redo it

Redo the questionnaire if a release adds any of these:

- user-to-user chat between shops or customers
- in-app purchases
- ads
- a web browser view
- location sharing
